# Why the adviser desk has no front end

The useful work is reading related records, recording verified outcomes and preparing the weekly review. A staff operator can do that through a coding agent with the supplied commands. Read-only HTML gives the team a printable caseload, follow-up desk and paperwork.

A student portal offers self-service bookings, mobile access, uploads and individual permissions. This base does not provide those. It has no offline synchronisation, real-time collaboration or drag-and-drop workflow. Enterprise DNA can scope those surfaces and connections in a custom version. The free base remains a staff record system that is useful on its own.

It does not replace a full student information system or all of Education Cloud. Admissions, recruitment, examinations, grades, clinical care, financial aid, payments and emergency handling remain with their existing systems and responsible people.
